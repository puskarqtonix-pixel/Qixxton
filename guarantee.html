MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE

Confidence backed by a clear guarantee.

Qixton believes in long-term relationships. If the agreed service scope is not delivered and you remain unsatisfied after a correction round, eligible Qixton service fees may be refunded under the conditions below.

What is covered

  • Eligible Qixton service fees for agreed scope items.
  • Services that do not match the approved scope after a correction attempt.
  • Requests raised within the 60-day guarantee window.

What is not covered

  • Ad spend and third-party platform charges.
  • Domain, hosting, plugin or software costs.
  • Taxes, payment processing fees and gateway charges.
  • Delays caused by missing content, approvals or access.

How to request a refund

  • Email Qixton with your project name and issue.
  • Explain what part of the approved scope was not met.
  • Allow one reasonable correction round.
  • If the issue remains unresolved and qualifies, a refund is processed for eligible service fees.

Relationship-first approach

Our first goal is always to fix the issue. The guarantee exists to build trust, but our preference is a long-term working relationship and a result both sides are satisfied with.
