If the agreed service scope is not delivered and you remain unsatisfied after one reasonable correction round, eligible Qixton service fees may be refundable under these terms.

What is covered

  • Eligible Qixton service fees for approved scope items.
  • Work that does not match the agreed scope after a correction attempt.
  • Requests submitted within the written guarantee window.

What is not covered

  • Ad spend or media budget.
  • Domains, hosting or third-party software.
  • Taxes, processor fees or platform fees.
  • Delays caused by missing access, content or client approvals.

How it works

  1. Tell us the issue.
  2. Compare it with the agreed scope.
  3. Allow one reasonable correction round.
  4. If unresolved and eligible, the applicable service fee can be refunded.

Final project-specific terms should also be written into the signed proposal or agreement. Third-party costs cannot be refunded by Qixton if they have already been paid to another provider.
