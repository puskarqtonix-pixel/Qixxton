60-DAY SERVICE SATISFACTION GUARANTEE — TERMS APPLY

Clear expectations. A fair correction process.

This is not an unconditional refund of every project expense. It is a service-satisfaction commitment covering eligible Qixton service fees when agreed scope remains unmet after a reasonable correction attempt.

When it can apply

  • You raise the issue within 60 days of the relevant service start/delivery date unless your signed proposal states another period.
  • The concern relates to a deliverable or service obligation clearly included in the agreed written scope.
  • Qixton is given a reasonable opportunity to correct the issue.
  • The issue remains unresolved after that correction attempt.

Potentially refundable

Eligible Qixton professional/service fees directly connected to the unresolved scope item, subject to the written project agreement and work already accepted or used.

Not refundable under this guarantee

  • Google, Meta, Amazon or other advertising/media spend
  • Domains, hosting, servers, SSL, software, plugins or subscriptions
  • Taxes, payment-processing or gateway fees
  • Third-party marketplace/platform charges
  • Delays caused by missing client content, approvals, credentials or access
  • New work or changes outside the agreed scope

How to request a review

  1. Send the project name and the specific scope item you believe is unresolved.
  2. Share supporting details/screenshots where useful.
  3. Allow the agreed correction round.
  4. If the issue remains and qualifies, Qixton reviews the eligible service-fee amount.

Important: Your signed proposal/contract controls if it contains different or more specific refund terms.
