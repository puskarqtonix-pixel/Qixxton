MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE

Confidence backed by a clear guarantee.

Qixton believes in long-term relationships. If the agreed service scope is not delivered and you remain unsatisfied after one correction round within the guarantee window, eligible Qixton service fees may be refunded under the conditions below.

What is covered

  • Eligible Qixton service fees for approved scope items.
  • Website, SEO, social media or other service work that does not match the agreed scope after a correction attempt.
  • Requests raised within the stated guarantee period.

Guarantee window

The homepage highlights a 60-day money-back guarantee. Specific projects can use a different written guarantee window if stated in the signed proposal or invoice.

What is not covered

  • Domain, hosting, SSL or server costs.
  • Ad spend on Google Ads, Meta Ads, Amazon Ads or other platforms.
  • Taxes, payment gateway charges or processor fees.
  • Third-party software, plugins, tools or subscriptions.
  • Work delayed by missing client content, approvals or access.

How to request a refund

  • Email Qixton with your project name and the issue.
  • Describe what part of the agreed scope was not met.
  • Allow one reasonable correction round.
  • If the issue remains unresolved and qualifies, a refund is processed for eligible service fees.

Relationship-first approach

Qixton prefers fixing the issue before discussing a refund. The goal is a long-term working relationship, clear communication and a result both sides are satisfied with.

Note: This page is a general website summary. Final refund terms should also be included in your signed proposal or agreement for full clarity.
