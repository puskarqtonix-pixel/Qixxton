HOW IT WORKS

Three steps.
No confusing fine print.

The guarantee is designed to be fair to the client and to the people doing the work.

01

Tell us what is wrong

Contact us within 7 calendar days of the first eligible deliverable and explain what does not meet the agreed scope or expectations.

02

Give us one chance to fix it

We will revise or correct the eligible work at no additional Qixton service charge within the agreed revision scope.

03

Still not satisfied?

If the eligible work still does not meet the agreed scope after that correction, you may request a refund of the eligible Qixton professional service fee paid for that scope.

✓

  • Qixton website design / development service fees before final approval and handover
  • First-month service fees for eligible new recurring marketing engagements when requested within the stated window
  • Work that materially fails to match the written scope after the included correction opportunity
×

Not refundable

  • Advertising spend paid to Google, Meta, Amazon or other platforms
  • Domain, hosting, marketplace, software, premium plugin or third-party charges
  • Taxes, bank charges, payment-processing fees or currency-conversion costs
  • Work or milestones already approved in writing
  • Custom assets, licenses or purchases that cannot be returned
  • Requests caused by a change of mind or new scope after approval
IMPORTANT DETAILS

Clear expectations protect both sides.

7-day window

The request should be submitted within 7 calendar days of the first eligible deliverable or the start of an eligible first-month service period.

Written scope controls

Your signed proposal, email approval or statement of work defines what was promised. The guarantee does not automatically add services that were not included.

Final approval ends eligibility

Once a deliverable is approved, handed over, published as final, or a completed milestone is accepted, that approved portion is not refundable.

Results cannot be guaranteed

SEO rankings, ad performance, social growth, marketplace sales and revenue depend on factors outside Qixton’s control. The guarantee covers eligible service delivery, not a guaranteed business outcome.

Plain-language summary: If eligible Qixton work does not meet the agreed scope, tell us quickly, let us correct it once, and if it still falls short you can request the eligible service fee back. Third-party money already spent is excluded.
