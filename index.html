FAQ

Questions before we start?

Quick answers about Qixton, our services and how we work with growing businesses.

What services does Qixton provide?+

Qixton provides custom website design and development, SEO and local SEO, social media marketing, Google and Meta advertising, online reputation support, and AI search optimization including AEO and GEO.

Do you build custom websites or use templates?+

Our website projects are built around the business, brand and conversion goals. We focus on responsive design, clear user journeys, performance and an SEO-ready structure rather than simply dropping content into a generic layout.

Can Qixton help a local or small business?+

Yes. We work with local businesses, startups and growing brands. The strategy can focus on local search visibility, enquiries, bookings, calls, social growth or paid lead generation depending on the business goal.

How long does SEO take to show results?+

SEO is a long-term growth channel rather than an instant result. Timing depends on your website, competition, location, search demand and current authority. We focus on measurable improvements in visibility, relevant traffic and enquiries instead of guaranteeing a specific ranking date.

Do you manage Google Ads and Meta Ads?+

Yes. Qixton can plan and manage paid campaigns across Google, Facebook and Instagram, including targeting, campaign structure, creative direction, landing-page alignment and performance tracking.

What are AEO and GEO?+

AEO and GEO help make your business content easier for search engines and AI-assisted discovery systems to understand. This can include structured content, schema, entity clarity, useful FAQs and stronger authority signals.

Can I choose only one service?+

Yes. You can start with a single service such as website design, SEO, social media or paid ads, or combine services when an integrated strategy makes more sense for your goals.

How do I get started with Qixton?+

Start by sending us your business details and what you want to improve. We can review your current online presence, discuss the goal and recommend an appropriate service or package before the project begins.
